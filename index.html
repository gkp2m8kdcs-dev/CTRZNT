Capabilities

Everything a serious investor journal needs.

Built from scratch with SwiftUI Canvas, CTRZNT delivers fast, beautiful charts and a workspace designed for the way you actually trade.

📈

Hand-Drawn Charts

Candlesticks with wicks, bodies, and live crosshairs; line charts with gradient fills; mountain charts with shaded ridges. Drag to scrub across six intervals from 1D to 5Y.

🌍

Global Markets

US, Hong Kong, China, Europe, Japan, ETFs, REITs, crypto, and commodities — filter by region, sector, or asset class.

📓

Trade Journal

Log every buy and sell with cost basis, realized and unrealized gains, notes, and photo attachments for receipts and statements.

🧮

Risk Analytics

Concentration via HHI, asset class and sector exposure, realized volatility per position, simulated worst drawdown, and annualized return.

📰

Market Insights

Curated headlines with tickers, categories, and a mood panel summarizing breadth, volatility, and a watchlist-derived fear/greed reading.

🎓

Education Library

A growing library of short lessons on allocation, DCA, risk, chart reading, taxes, and investor behavior.

⭐

Smart Watchlist

Starred names one tap away, with custom tags and quick price chips for fast triage across sessions.

🔁

Converter & Compare

A multi-currency converter and a comparison view with normalized performance, correlation matrix, and stats.

📤

CSV Export

Export your full trade journal to CSV anytime. Your data, your format, your machine — never a server.

Charts that feel alive.

Every chart is rendered from scratch with SwiftUI Canvas — no third-party libraries, no slowdowns, no compromises. Candlesticks with proper wicks and bodies, line charts with gradient fills and end-of-series dots, mountain charts with peak-to-valley ridges.

  • Drag to scrub history across six intervals
  • Live OHLC crosshair with volume readout
  • Smooth 60fps interaction on supported devices

Tools for the way you actually trade.

Beyond charts, CTRZNT is a full workspace: filterable markets directory, sector heatmap, normalized comparison view, correlation matrix, and a multi-currency converter. All the analytical scaffolding a private investor needs.

  • Sector / region / asset class heatmap
  • Normalized performance comparison
  • Correlation matrix and per-pair stats

Privacy

Local-only by design.

No accounts. No logins. No analytics. No tracking. No notifications. No biometric prompts. Every trade, note, photo, and receipt stays on your device, under your control.

Your data never leaves your phone.

CTRZNT does not transmit your portfolio, trades, or personal information to any server. The only network access is for the market data feed, which is anonymous and contains no user identifiers. You can review the full details in our Privacy Policy.
