Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

Last updated: January 1, 2026

CTRZNT ("we", "our", "us") is designed from the ground up to be a private investor journal. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what data CTRZNT (also marketed as Portfolio Horizon Pro) does and does not collect.

1. Summary in one paragraph

CTRZNT is a local-only application. We do not require an account. We do not log you in. We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or any personal information. Your trades, notes, photos, watchlists, and settings live on your device. The only network traffic originates from anonymous market data requests that contain no user identifier.

2. Data we collect

Strictly speaking, CTRZNT does not collect data from you. The data you create in the app — trades, holdings, notes, watchlist entries, education progress, journal photos — is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us.

The only data that leaves your device is:

  • Anonymous market data requests. When you open the Markets tab or refresh prices, CTRZNT requests public market data (quotes, OHLC, volume) from our data provider. These requests contain no user identifier, no advertising ID, and no device fingerprint — only the symbol and timeframe you are viewing.
  • Optional support correspondence. If you email us at Mefepzavap@icloud.com, we receive whatever information you choose to include in your message. We use it only to respond to you.

3. Data we do not collect

CTRZNT does not collect any of the following. This list is exhaustive.

  • Name, email address, phone number, or other contact information (unless you email us)
  • Account credentials of any kind
  • Your trades, holdings, cost basis, gains, or losses
  • Your notes, photos, or journal entries
  • Your watchlists, tags, or education progress
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID)
  • Device identifiers used for tracking
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Contacts, calendars, or other on-device data not used by CTRZNT
  • Biometric data (Face ID / Touch ID are not used or prompted)
  • Crash reports, analytics events, or session recordings
  • Push notification tokens (notifications are not used)

4. How your data is stored

All user-generated content — trades, holdings, notes, photos, watchlists, settings, education progress — is stored locally in the app's sandboxed storage area on your iOS device. This storage is:

  • Sandboxed. Other apps cannot read it.
  • Backed up by iCloud and iTunes/Finder backups if you have enabled device backups. We do not control these backups; they are managed entirely by Apple's iOS backup system. If you disable backups or use encrypted backups, your CTRZNT data follows the same rule.
  • Removed when you delete the app. Uninstalling CTRZNT deletes all locally stored data.

Photos you attach to trades are copied into the app's sandbox. We do not modify or read your broader photo library.

5. iOS permissions we request

CTRZNT requests only the permissions it needs to provide the features you use. Each prompt explains why the permission is required:

  • Photo Library (read): "CTRZNT needs photo library access so you can attach receipts, statements, and notes to your trade records." Used only when you explicitly choose a photo to attach to a trade.
  • Camera: "CTRZNT uses the camera so you can capture receipts and documents for your trade entries." Used only when you tap the camera action on a trade.
  • Photo Library (write): "CTRZNT can save generated chart cards and exported reports to your photo library." Used only when you tap the export-to-photos action.

You can deny any permission and continue using the rest of the app. Photos and camera features are optional.

CTRZNT does not request access to contacts, calendars, microphone, location, motion, Face ID, Touch ID, Bluetooth, or any background mode.

6. Third-party services

CTRZNT uses a third-party market data provider to deliver price quotes and OHLC history. The provider receives only the symbol and timeframe you are viewing; it does not receive your device identifier, your user identifier (because you have none), or any content you have stored in CTRZNT.

We do not integrate advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, attribution SDKs, social login SDKs, or any other third-party tracking technology.

7. Children's privacy

CTRZNT is intended for adults and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because CTRZNT does not collect personal information from anyone, this policy applies uniformly regardless of the user's age.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Because CTRZNT has no account system and does not contact users directly, we encourage you to review this page periodically.

Material changes will never reduce the privacy protections described above without your explicit consent.
