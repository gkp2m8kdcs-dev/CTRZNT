Frequently Asked Questions

Does CTRZNT require an account?

+
No. CTRZNT is fully usable without an account. There is no sign-up, no login, and no email verification.

Where is my data stored?

+
All trades, notes, photos, watchlists, and settings are stored locally in the app's sandbox on your iOS device. If you have iCloud or iTunes backups enabled, your CTRZNT data is included in those backups according to your system settings.

How do I back up or move my data?

+
CTRZNT relies on the standard iOS device backup mechanism. For trade history specifically, you can use the in-app CSV export to save a portable copy of your journal at any time.

Can I attach photos to my trades?

+
Yes. Open any trade and use the camera or photo library action to attach receipts, statements, or notes. Photos are copied into the app's sandbox and remain on your device.

Which markets and asset classes are supported?

+
CTRZNT covers US, Hong Kong, China, Europe, Japan, ETFs, REITs, crypto, and commodities. Filter the Markets directory by region, sector, or asset class to find what you trade.

Does CTRZNT provide investment advice?

+
No. CTRZNT is a journaling and analytical workspace. It does not provide brokerage services, place trades, or recommend securities. All content is for informational and educational purposes only and is not financial advice.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

+
Email Mefepzavap@icloud.com with a description and, if possible, steps to reproduce. Screenshots are always appreciated.

How do I delete all my CTRZNT data?

+
Uninstall the app. Because CTRZNT stores everything in its sandboxed container, deleting the app removes all locally stored CTRZNT data from your device.
